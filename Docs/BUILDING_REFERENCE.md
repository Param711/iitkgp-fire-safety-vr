# Building reference and limitations

The environment is a reconstruction informed by descriptions and exterior photographs of IIT Kharagpur's Industrial and Systems Engineering department. The photographs and video are not part of the public repository.

Confirmed layout inputs:

- One main staircase.
- Main ground entrance opposite the stairs, approximately four metres wide.
- A second ground-floor exit to the right when facing the lift doors.
- A water cooler opposite the lift on each floor.
- At the washroom branch, the women's door is straight ahead and the men's is to the right.

The generated environment has three main floors, 28 accessible spaces, six washrooms, three coolers and 12 occupants. Exit offset/width, corridor distances, individual room dimensions, some interiors and the assembly location remain provisional. The exterior uses architectural cues rather than a surveyed digital twin.

The model is a teaching and assessment prototype. It is not CFD, a certified evacuation model, a verified crowd simulation, an emergency-response system or evidence of compliance with local building/fire requirements. Fire-development parameters, agent compatibility rules, training distances and scoring are simplified and should be reviewed by the course instructor and relevant local safety personnel.

The actual extinguisher agent/rating and local blocked-route procedures still need verification. Closed offices are not certified refuges, and waiting for assistance is not scored as a completed rescue. Proposed WSDAE experiments such as controlled Fitts/Hick tasks, formal PMTS and work-sampling studies are not implemented or validated in this source release.
